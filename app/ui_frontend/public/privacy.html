Privacy

Effective September 19, 2026

InsiderTrack is self-hosted software run by the operator of this site. There are no user accounts. This page lists everything the site stores about a visitor and why.

1. Information stored

  • Terms acknowledgement. When you accept the terms on first visit, the site records the time, your IP address, your browser's user-agent string and — only if you typed one — your email address. This is the operator's record that the not-financial-advice notice was shown.
  • Watchlist. If you save tickers with an email address, the tickers and that address are stored, together with a random access token kept in your browser. No password is set.
  • Email reports and alerts. If you subscribe, your address and your report and alert preferences are stored so the emails can be sent. Every email has an unsubscribe path.
  • Server logs. Standard request logs (path, time, status, IP) are kept in the container's log for operational troubleshooting and rotate automatically.

Everything else on the site — trades, filings, holdings, prices, scores — is public information about public figures and companies, not about you.

2. Browser storage

The site keeps a few things in your browser only: that you accepted the terms, your theme choice, your watchlist email and token, and — if you set one — your own AI provider key. None of these are sent anywhere except this site's own API, and your AI key is sent only with the requests you make and is never stored or logged on the server.

3. AI processing

Research notes on ticker pages are generated by an AI provider (Claude, Gemini or OpenAI) from the public data shown on the page. Nothing about you is included in those requests. Scanned government filings are read by the same kind of model; those requests contain the public filing and nothing else.

4. Cookies

One cookie exists, and only for the site operator: the admin session, set after entering the admin password, expiring after an hour. Visitors get no cookies.

5. Backups

The operator keeps encrypted backups of the database, which include the watchlist and subscriber records above. Backups are retained on a rolling schedule and are readable only with the operator's key.

6. Your choices

  • Remove your watchlist and email from Settings → Watchlist; unsubscribe from any report email or from Settings → Email reports.
  • Clear the site's browser storage to forget the terms acknowledgement, theme and any AI key.
  • Ask the operator to delete the terms-acknowledgement record tied to your email or address.

7. Contact

Questions about this page or the data go to the site operator, M.G. Network and Technology Solutions.
